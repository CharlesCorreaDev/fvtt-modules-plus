# Foundry VTT Knowledge Base: Scenes, Lighting & Dynamic Vision

> Source: [https://foundryvtt.com/kb/](https://foundryvtt.com/kb/)

## 1. Scene Configuration
- **Grids:** Square, Hexagonal (Column/Row, Pointy/Flat), and Gridless.
- **Dimensions & Padding:** Default 100px per grid square.

## 2. Dynamic Lighting & Vision Engine
- **Walls:** Normal walls (block light/vision/movement), Doors (secret, locked), Windows (block movement, allow vision/light), Ethereal walls.
- **Lights:** Ambient Light sources with customizable radius, color, intensity, and animation presets (torch, pulse, chromatic, sunburst).
- **Vision Modes:** Darkvision, Tremorsense, Blindsight, Blindness, and Inverted Vision.
