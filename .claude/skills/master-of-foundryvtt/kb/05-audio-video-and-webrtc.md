# Foundry VTT Knowledge Base: Audio, Video & WebRTC

> Source: [https://foundryvtt.com/kb/](https://foundryvtt.com/kb/)

## 1. Audio Playlists & Ambient Audio
- **Playlists:** Sequential, Shuffle, Simultaneous playback.
- **Ambient Sounds:** Radius-based positional audio sources on canvas.

## 2. Integrated WebRTC Voice & Video
- Requires valid SSL certificate (`https://`).
- Built-in peer-to-peer WebRTC conferencing with LiveKit / Jitsi external server support.
