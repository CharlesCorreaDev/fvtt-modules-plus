# Arquitetura dos 10 Subagentes Especializados — Bootstrap UX/UI Plus 🎨⚡
## 10 Specialized Subagents Architecture — Bootstrap UX/UI Plus 🎨⚡

A suíte **Bootstrap UX/UI Plus** opera sob o padrão de Engenharia Federada em Linha de Montagem, garantindo interfaces visualmente impecáveis, responsivas (Bootstrap 5.3 & 6.0), acessíveis (WCAG 2.2 AA) e ergonomicamente confortáveis para neurodivergentes (TDAH & TEA).

The **Bootstrap UX/UI Plus** suite operates under a federated assembly line engineering model, ensuring visually polished, fully responsive (Bootstrap 5.3 & 6.0), accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA), and cognitively ergonomic interfaces tailored for neurodiversity (ADHD & Autism Spectrum).

`mermaid
flowchart TD
    User([Usuário / Desenvolvedor / User]) --> Showrunner[1. Diretor de Arte & Orquestrador UX/UI]
    Showrunner --> SpecUxHandoff[2. Engenheiro de Specs, PRs, SSD & Handoff Design System]
    SpecUxHandoff --> UxArchitect[3. Arquiteto de Leis de UX Fitts/Hick/Miller]
    UxArchitect --> NeuroErgonomist[3. Neurodiversidade TDAH/TEA]
    UxArchitect --> ColorPsychologist[4. Psicologia das Cores Eva Heller]
    NeuroErgonomist & ColorPsychologist --> ComponentDev[5. Front-end Bootstrap & CSS Scoped]
    ComponentDev --> ResponsiveArchitect[6. Responsividade & Mobile-First]
    ComponentDev & ResponsiveArchitect --> AccessibilityAuditor[7. Acessibilidade WCAG 2.2 AA]
    AccessibilityAuditor --> MultiFrameworkDev[8. Adaptador Multi-Framework]
    MultiFrameworkDev --> DesignSigner[9. Auditor de Design Anti-Slop]
    DesignSigner --> TechnicalWriter[10. Documentador Design System EN/PT-BR]
    TechnicalWriter --> UxArchitect
    UxArchitect --> OutputFinal([Interface Impecável, Acessível e Responsiva])
`

## 🇧🇷 Português (Brasil) — Papéis e Responsabilidades dos 11 Subagentes

1. **Diretor de Arte & Orquestrador UX/UI (Showrunner):** Entrevista o usuário via grill-me, define identidade visual e gerencia os ciclos dos subagentes.
2. **Engenheiro de Specs, PRs, SSD & Handoff (Memória de UI/Design System):** Centraliza especificações de UI/UX, diagramas de fluxo de tela (SSD), descrições de PRs e manuais de handoff para desenvolvedores front-end.

1. **Diretor de Arte & Orquestrador UX/UI (Showrunner):** Entrevista o usuário via grill-me, define identidade visual e gerencia os ciclos dos subagentes.
2. **Arquiteto de Leis de UX & Heurísticas:** Aplica as Leis de UX (Hick, Fitts, Miller, Jakob, Zeigarnik) e as 10 Heurísticas de Jakob Nielsen.
3. **Engenheiro de Neurodiversidade (TDAH & Espectro Autista):** Elimina sobrecarga cognitiva, aplica micro-passos visuais, contraste suave e bionic bolding.
4. **Especialista em Psicologia das Cores:** Seleciona paletas intencionais baseadas na Psicologia das Cores de Eva Heller com harmonia sensorial.
5. **Desenvolvedor Front-end Bootstrap & CSS Scoped:** Constrói layouts com Bootstrap 5.3/6.0 com isolamento estrito de classes para evitar vazamento de estilos no DOM.
6. **Especialista em Responsividade & Mobile-First:** Garante comportamento impecável em telas mobile, tablets, desktops e ultrawide.
7. **Auditor de Acessibilidade WCAG 2.2 AA:** Valida contraste de cores (mínimo 4.5:1), navegação por teclado e semântica ARIA completa.
8. **Adaptador Multi-Framework:** Converte componentes para PHP/Blade, Twig, React, Python/Django e HTML puro.
9. **Auditor Geral de Design & Anti-Slop (Portão Obrigatório):** Elimina layouts artificiais genéricos, garantindo respiro e hierarquia visual refinada.
10. **Redator Técnico & Documentador de Design System:** Cria o guia de estilo, documentação de componentes e snippets em Português e Inglês conforme [SemVer 2.0.0 PT-BR](https://semver.org/lang/pt-BR/).

## 🇺🇸 English — Roles and Responsibilities of the 11 Subagents

1. **Art Director & UX/UI Orchestrator (Showrunner):** Interviews stakeholders via grill-me, establishes design systems, and orchestrates creative turns.
2. **Specs, PRs, SSD & Handoff Engineer (Design System Memory):** Centralizes UI/UX specifications, user interaction sequence diagrams (SSD), PR manifests, and frontend handoff guides.

1. **Art Director & UX/UI Orchestrator (Showrunner):** Interviews stakeholders via grill-me, establishes design systems, and orchestrates creative turns.
2. **Laws of UX & Heuristics Architect:** Applies cognitive UX Laws (Hick, Fitts, Miller, Jakob, Zeigarnik) and Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics.
3. **Neurodiversity Ergonomist (ADHD & Autism):** Eliminates decision fatigue, applies cognitive micro-steps, soothing contrast, and bionic bolding.
4. **Color Psychologist (Eva Heller Principles):** Formulates intentional palettes according to psychological associations and sensory comfort.
5. **Frontend Developer (Bootstrap & Scoped CSS):** Authors clean component markup in Bootstrap 5.3/6.0 with strict CSS namespacing to avoid host DOM bleeding.
6. **Responsive Design & Mobile-First Specialist:** Ensures flawless layout adaptations across phones, tablets, laptops, and ultrawide displays.
7. **Accessibility Auditor (WCAG 2.2 AA & ARIA):** Validates minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratios, screen reader compatibility, and full keyboard navigation.
8. **Multi-Framework Adapter:** Maps and exports components into PHP/Blade, Twig, React, Python/Django, and clean vanilla HTML.
9. **Anti-Slop Visual Quality Auditor (Mandatory Quality Gate):** Eliminates generic AI aesthetics, enforcing deliberate whitespace and typographic hierarchy.
10. **Bilingual Design System Technical Writer (EN / PT-BR):** Produces style guides, token definitions, and reusable code snippets in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
