# Foundry VTT Knowledge Base: Compendiums & Data Management

> Source: [https://foundryvtt.com/kb/](https://foundryvtt.com/kb/)

## 1. Compendium Architecture
- Database packs stored in binary LevelDB format (v11+).
- Dramatically lowers world loading times by loading documents on-demand.

## 2. World Backups
- Complete backup of `Data/worlds/<world-name>/` directory.
